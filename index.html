01.2 Private VouchersWith Nikon Marketing

A sharp price for the right buyer, without a public price war.

In August a public Flash Sale at ₫19.2–19.5M on the Z30 triggered dealer complaints. We moved the deep discount into a Private Voucher: the listed price stays safe, and only buyers who receive the code from Nikon events, livestreams or social DMs get the final price.

01.4 Peak-hour adsShopee Ads

Small budget, fired only when traffic peaks.

Budgets are pulsed: bids go up on double days, mid-month (15) and payday (25) during the high-impression hours, and are held back the rest of the month. Product ads with custom ROAS targets cover the hero SKUs, and GMV Max auto-bidding covers the shop.

AppendixHow this deck is built

UX/UI implementation plan, after Apple Liquid Glass

This page follows Apple's Liquid Glass guidance: glass is a separate functional layer for navigation and controls that floats above the content, used sparingly. The motion vocabulary takes cues from product launch pages such as OXVA's: pinned storytelling, horizontal galleries and count-ups.

1 · Hierarchy

Glass only on the navigation layer

The dock, segmented controls, tooltips and sheets are glass. Data panels stay solid so numbers keep full contrast. Applies "avoid overusing Liquid Glass effects".

2 · Navigation

Floating dock that minimises on scroll

The chapter dock shrinks to a counter when scrolling down and expands when scrolling up, like the iOS tab bar minimise behaviour. A yellow lens slides to the active chapter.

3 · Concentricity

Rounded shapes nest

Pills sit inside pills, and sheets and panels use one radius scale (12/20/28) so inner corners stay concentric with their containers.

4 · Colour

Colour belongs to content

Controls are neutral glass. Nikon yellow marks the active state, the 2026 series and the one number that matters on each slide.

5 · Motion

One signature moment, then restraint

An aperture-iris opening and a viewfinder HUD in the hero. After that, scroll-linked reveals, a pinned campaign wall and a pinned listing story.

6 · Accessibility

Respects system settings

Reduce Motion removes animation, and Reduce Transparency turns glass solid. Content is readable at rest, focus rings are visible, and charts carry text labels.

7 · Present mode

Meeting-room ready

Press P or the Present button for slide snapping. Arrow keys and Page Up/Down move between slides, and F goes full screen where the browser allows. On phones the dock turns into previous / next buttons and a chapter menu, and charts redraw at the screen's real width.

8 · Next iteration

Live data

Connect the charts to the Forecast sheet so numbers refresh before each meeting, and add October once the month closes.
